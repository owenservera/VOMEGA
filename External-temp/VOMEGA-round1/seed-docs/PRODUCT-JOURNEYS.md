# Product Journeys — Outcome Compass
These describe outcomes VIVIM should increasingly make possible without prescribing screens, schemas, components, or implementation order.

**J1 Open & Orient** — open VIVIM and understand the relevant current world: what exists, changed, matters, is active, and can happen next.

**J2 Continue Work** — return to a project and recover context, history, relationships, unfinished Work, decisions, and relevant material without reconstructing it manually.

**J3 Universal Interaction** — address VIVIM or something in the World, express intent in ordinary human terms, resolve material ambiguity, and reach appropriate capabilities without learning implementation vocabulary.

**J4 Delegated Action** — entrust an outcome, allow governed execution, and receive a truthful result with enough evidence to understand what happened.

**J5 Provider Choice** — use/control existing services, accounts, models, and capabilities; choices are visible/configurable when they matter rather than hidden inside opaque routing.

**J6 Background Continuity** — leave authorized Work running and later understand what completed, failed, changed, needs approval, or deserves attention.

**J7 Evolution** — ask VIVIM to gain, compose, modify, repair, or replace capability through ordinary evidence/governance rather than a privileged backdoor.

**J8 Exit & Recovery** — export, reconstruct, migrate, or replace the environment without surrendering identity/lineage of essential user-owned state.

Across journeys prefer simple human mental models, durable identity, inspectable authority, explicit uncertainty, real external results, replaceability, and recovery.

## MVP relationship
MVP is a steering mechanism, not a fixed feature list. Each cycle seeks the smallest genuinely useful, installable slice producing real user value and observable results while maximizing learning, reuse, time-to-market, and expansion potential. Small surface is desirable; fake completeness is not.
