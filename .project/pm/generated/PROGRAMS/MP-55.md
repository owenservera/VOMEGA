## Explanation

MP-55 builds Failure Capsules: compact, portable packages that capture a meaningful development failure as evidence rather than a narrative about it. A capsule records the exact invocation, process/environment versions, inputs, observed vs expected result, relevant file/diff state, the observed failure signature and a reproduction recipe. A worker or model that receives a capsule receives "this exact failure", not "something broke".

A capsule is deliberately not a log store, a screen recording, or a test suite. It is a selective, failure-scoped slice of reality with declared prerequisites and a replay path. The Failure Capsules hypothesis (DEVELOPMENT-ACCELERATION-HYPOTHESES.md #3) lists possible contents — command/tool invocation, stdout/stderr, file/diff hashes, test inputs, browser/provider state where safe, recent actions, failure signature, cleanup/reproduction instructions — and MP-55 turns that menu into a concrete artifact whose quality is measurable: can another worker reproduce and independently diagnose from it?

The program starts manual and becomes automatic. P1 freezes a failure envelope around a real invocation. P2 makes it replayable in a fresh worktree under declared prerequisites. P3 enriches capsules with MP-21 Reflection entity/test references instead of bare paths and text. P4 shapes a compact cross-agent diagnostic packet that a fresh agent can diagnose without reading the repository. P5 automates capture and minimizes capsules down to the smallest useful reproducer.

MP-55 is one of the five selected development accelerators. It feeds MP-56 (minimized capsules become trace-to-fixture and regression-harvesting material) and enriches MP-54 context bundles (MP54-P3 consumes MP55-G2). Capsules are evidence, not authority: diagnosis belongs to whoever consumes the capsule, and capture stays local and privacy-reduced by default.

## Problem — why this program exists

Without capsules, failures travel as narratives: "something broke" passes between workers, and each new worker re-diagnoses from scratch — re-running commands, guessing at environment and inputs, often failing to reproduce the failure at all. This persistently costs VOMEGA time (repeated manual reproduction), truth (diagnoses ungrounded in the actual observed failure), and capability (parallel workers blocked on failures they cannot hand off). Concrete reality: the harness note in .project/agentic-launch/STATUS.md records that unbounded workers stall — diagnosis without reproducible evidence is guesswork. It also throws away information the team later pays to reconstruct manually, exactly the waste the acceleration program-wide principle targets: development should leave useful evidence as a side effect instead of discarding it.

## Objectives

1. Capture meaningful failures as structured envelopes (invocation, environment versions, inputs, observed vs expected result, failure signature) without relying on agent memory or self-summary.
2. Reproduce a selected failure in a fresh worktree from a capsule under declared prerequisites, with no undeclared setup.
3. Bind capsules to task, commit, tests and — after MP21-G3 — dereferenceable Reflection entity/test references, not only paths and text.
4. Enable a fresh agent or model to produce a correct diagnosis from the diagnostic packet alone, without reading the repository.
5. Minimize capsules to the smallest useful reproducer and hand them to MP-56 so failures become fixture/regression material instead of being discarded.
6. Make capture cheap enough that leaving failure evidence is a normal side effect of development, not a discipline workers must remember.

## Boundaries / anti-goals

- Not a surveillance layer: no indiscriminate keystroke, screen, message, credential or content capture; any sensitive capture must be scoped, local, purposeful and reviewable (hypotheses privacy default).
- Privacy-reduced by default: credentials and secrets redacted, minimal raw content, local storage, bounded retention; material that cannot be safely reduced is not captured wholesale.
- Not a replacement for tests or Ratchet: capsules are evidence inputs, not proof or authority; a capsule replay is not a merge gate and does not override the Ratchet as execution/proof owner.
- Not a general log archive or a Development Reality Layer substitute: capsules are selective and failure-scoped, and most raw detail should expire rather than accumulate.
- Not a diagnosis engine: the capsule packages evidence; diagnosis belongs to the consuming worker/agent, which remains replaceable.
- Not a new repository-analysis system: after MP21-G3 it references MP-21 structural identities instead of rebuilding parsing or identity schemes.

## Success conditions

- Given only a P1 envelope, another worker names the exact failing command/input/exit as the original (MP55-G1).
- A capsule replayed in a clean worktree reproduces the failure signature with only declared prerequisites (MP55-G2).
- Capsule fields resolve to dereferenceable Reflection entity/test references (MP55-G3).
- An independent agent given only the diagnostic packet produces a diagnosis consistent with the known cause, without repo access (MP55-G4).
- Automatically captured capsules preserve the seeded failure signature byte-for-byte on the named fields (MP55-G5).
- Meaningful failures stop traveling as narratives in handoffs, and minimized capsules flow into MP-56 fixture/regression harvesting.

## Falsifiers / open questions

- If envelopes fail MP55-G1 (a second worker cannot identify the exact failing invocation), P1's capture shape is wrong and must be narrowed or redesigned.
- If capsules do not replay in fresh worktrees despite declared prerequisites, P2's reproducibility claim fails and work redirects toward environment-manifest mechanics (cf. hypothesis #22) or shrinks to envelope-only.
- If structural enrichment references cannot be dereferenced by consumers, or enrichment adds cost without diagnosis value, P3 is split or reduced.
- If independent agents consistently cannot diagnose from packets alone, P4's repo-free packet concept is falsified and must be redesigned around declared repo slices.
- If automatic capture adds measurable overhead, fires on noise, or minimization drops evidence the failure signature depends on, P5 is reduced or gated behind better triggers and redaction.
- If measured failure-reproduction and diagnosis time do not improve after adoption, MP-55 fails the accelerator earning rule (measurably reduce failure cost) and should be simplified or retired.
