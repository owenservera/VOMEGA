## Assignment

Study whether there is a universal **minimal Elephant genotype** capable of generating reliable, useful specialized Elephants regardless of domain. The initial 20 traits, five families and six phenotype tests are **seed hypotheses to challenge**, not a specification of twenty production components.

Develop falsifiable alternatives: fewer/better genes, improved taxonomy, independent operational self-models, multiple kinds of continuity, more effective peer cognition, distinct memory economics, or an unexpectedly simple wrapper-based architecture. Explicitly argue which traits are intrinsic, which require environmental instrumentation, and which are specialized phenotypes masquerading as genes.

Do not declare a universal gene because the Semantic Factory needs it. Test on at least three dissimilar domains; keep a genuine no-Elephant baseline. Test real context boundary separation and source freshness; do not claim model-level introspection exposes hidden inference state. Use full lifecycle resource attribution with UNKNOWN for missing costs. Measure real performance over model self-assessments.

## Starter experimental fan-out — hypotheses, not fixed departments

- **Track G — Genome challenger:** falsify/minimize the 20 genes; propose a smaller necessary/sufficient core and traits that must become optional.
- **Track I — Introspection challenger:** instrument measured/estimated/unknown resource state, coverage, calibration and reconstruction against self-report deception.
- **Track H — Herd challenger:** compare simple typed exchange against distributed/federated peer knowledge; measure coordination costs, contradictions and stale propagation.
- **Track M — Metabolism challenger:** compare hot resident, cold rebuild, snapshot plus retrieval and calibrated delta ingestion on equal tasks/epochs.
- **Track X — Open invention:** free to propose distinct schemas, tools, protocols, testing methods, and new traits; must include an original baseline and failure test.

These are experiment questions, **not required permanent team labels**, quotas, fixed models or guaranteed parallel agents. Use only as much genuine isolated fan-out as the available host and budget can prove. Other experiments may outperform them.

## First turn and end-of-turn obligations

1. Pin the exact VOMEGA SHA, source/research cohort and the DNA seed version. Log any code/data sources you actually inspected and what remains unread.
2. Classify genes as **UNIVERSAL_CANDIDATE**, **SPECIALIZED**, **ENVIRONMENTAL_SUPPORT**, **DUPLICATE**, **REJECTED** or **UNKNOWN**, with explicit reason and falsifier. Do not approve universality by a vote.
3. Pick one or two genes for the **smallest executable test**. Make a controlled observation, report all missed tests, and preserve negative results.
4. Propose one independently useful, bounded tool/protocol improvement only if it addresses observed friction. Assess build vs reuse; compare against the simplest baseline.
5. Publish the full capability-pack portfolio **after every top-level turn**, including blocked, unchanged, failed and immature content, with hashes and external reconstructability according to [every-turn publication contract](../01_EXPECTED_OUTPUTS/02_EVERY_TURN_PUBLICATION_CONTRACT.md).
6. Return a proposed genome diff, what evidence would overturn it, and what (if anything) can responsibly be promoted into a shared obligation. No silent changes to product code, experiment baselines, Git main, live provider accounts or owner policy.

## Promotion test

A universal gene earns a recommendation only after at least two competing implementations achieve its observable phenotype over distinct use cases, with reproducible independent scoring and quantified overhead. A promoted implementation mechanism needs separate evidence that enforcing it universally beats permitting alternatives. A proposal never becomes constitutional law without explicit owner acceptance.

Continue to permit Z.ai inventing new methods or genes if tests reveal gaps. The desired output is a portable **conceptual genome and observable competence probes**, not another preselected orchestration stack.
