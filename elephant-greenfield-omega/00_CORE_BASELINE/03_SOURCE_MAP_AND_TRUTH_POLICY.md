# Baseline Sources, Authority and Migration

## Canonical ownership
- **Shared vision/problem/requirements:** this 00_CORE_BASELINE folder, plus 01_EXPECTED_OUTPUTS normative requirements.
- **Existing historic Elephant hypothesis:** ../01_MASTER_CHARTER.md, ../protocol/ and ../reference/SOURCES.md. This is prior design work and specifically the basis for **02_ARCHITECTURE_PROPOSALS/ours**, not the binding architecture for Claude or other proposals.
- **Existing operational Z.ai exploratory mandate:** ../BOOTSTRAP-ZAI-EXPERIMENT-LAB/ (preserved). Its original two-master/four-arm and later C sidecar remain the general lab's **active Round 1** governance.
- **New proposal-scoped development:** ../03_DEVELOPMENT_SYSTEMS/proposal-specific/ describes separate full Z.ai sessions, which must not pretend to be instances of the same live general Z.ai conversation.
- **Publications:** ../04_TURN_PUBLICATIONS/ is a required receiving/lookup convention; a running system can choose another layout only if the same release and discoverability contract is met.

## Who can change what
The product seed changes only through a dated baseline revision and fair benchmark re-freeze. Each proposal author owns its separate speculative design. Each development system owns its *own* runtime, evidence and candidate pack portfolios in its isolated execution workspace. Integration to one consolidated repo main occurs through reviewed, conflict-aware updates; use temporary worktrees, not standing owner branches.

The lab must preserve actual round/version/source hashes. Archived documents and mock test output are not proofs of live provider connectivity or actual session persistence. Every status and benchmark claim needs direct evidence.

## Launch package for independent proposal writers
Give only 00_CORE_BASELINE, 01_EXPECTED_OUTPUTS, and the proposal-creation charter in 02_ARCHITECTURE_PROPOSALS. Do NOT supply "ours" protocol design as a constraint if genuine independent architectural diversity is the goal. Authors may inspect prior art with attribution but their first proposal should be created from the common neutral charter.

## Legacy compatibility policy
**Additive non-destructive transition**: do not move or delete old paths while live Z.ai Round 1 may be using them. New folders are the official project information architecture, and old paths remain stable for any already-running session. The existence of both is deliberate. Future cleanup is a separate audited cutover after live sessions switch.
