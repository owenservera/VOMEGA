# Elephant Project — Shared Vision and Boundaries

## Why this exists
Ordinary code-writing agents repeatedly reconstruct architecture, dependencies, intent and history from identical repositories. We want to test the proposition that retained or reconstructable domain-aware cognitive contexts—**Elephants**—can become on-demand sources of actionable, verifiable guidance to isolated coding workers. We do not assume that durable chat sessions win; repeated fresh-context retrieval, standard tool ecosystems or an unforeseen mechanism may win after measurement.

**End goal:** a provider-agnostic, portable, source-verifiable method for AI development that increases **independently accepted useful software produced per full lifecycle time and resource cost**, improves correctness and recovery, and reveals worthwhile novel technical methods. Build an empirical system, not an impressive diagram.

## Shared research levels
- **E0:** normal coding workers and tools, no retained Elephant cognition. Mandatory meaningful control.
- **E1:** one genuine contextual Elephant cognition resource with verified effective source basis, explicit calls, provenance and recovery.
- **E2:** two separate simultaneously usable Elephant cognition resources with cross-boundary collaboration, disagreement detection, authentic context separation and full coordination economics.
The number of architecture proposals, developer sessions or A/B/C research teams does not count as the number of Elephants.

## What is fixed about shape
Canonical source/accepted commits, tests and observed runtime results outrank the model's remembered or asserted context. Model work is fallible and must carry source and version basis. No silent privilege escalation, hidden source modifications, credential exports, invented model capabilities or fabricated throughput/tokens. Isolated agents and genuine parallel execution must be proven rather than simulated. Every hypothesis is falsifiable and retained with honest evidence.

Workers can edit their own isolated workspaces under permissions. Elephant code patches, if tested, require independently verifiable owner/reviewer controls. Unattended self-modification, third-party source uploads and live user account automation are outside the frozen initial tests unless separately authorized.

## Expected output philosophy
The final component inventory is **not predesigned**. Each proposal author may choose its own conceptual architecture. Each independent development system may discover capabilities and pack boundaries during execution. Every track must publish **all its owned packs each development turn regardless of maturity**, with accurate state and evidence; no quotas and no suppression of negative results.

## Research independence
Different architecture authors (our initial ECP hypothesis, Claude, other models) receive identical shared baseline and common expected-output requirements and produce separately attributable designs. A **general unrestricted Z.ai research lab** can explore architectural hypotheses. A **proposal-scoped Z.ai instance** explores and optimizes one author's declared design, with scoped independent fan-out and transparent departures from its anchor. Runs share evaluation conditions where feasible but not live memory or writable worktrees.

## Honest experimental economics
Include model inference and reasoning token measurements when provider reports them, cached tokens without double count, failed calls, setup, pack preparation, context loading/flush, tool/process time, installation, integration, evaluators and retries. Unknown counters are UNKNOWN, never zero. Do not infer causal speedups from one unpaired task.
