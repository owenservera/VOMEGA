# Handoff, Reproducibility and Evidence Acceptance

## External handoff
At each meaningful milestone independently initialize an agent with **only a track publication and declared dependencies**. It must identify the complete pack inventory, distinguish VERIFIED from DISCOVERED, retrieve versioned materials, reconstruct tests or operating methods, detect absent evidence, recover from a relevant failure and report integration risks. Zero permission to consult the originating agent session is part of the test.

## Verification levels
- **Publication integrity:** track's own complete catalog and immutable pack artifact links resolve and match hashes. **Required every turn.**
- **Experiment authenticity:** child tool/model calls, source diffs, test outputs, session identity and real isolation are independently observed. Not implied by published docs.
- **Pack verification:** independently reproduce bounded functionality/claim, including negatives. State maturity separately.
- **Portfolio operational proof:** runnable track reference implementation from clean environment when claimed.
- **Cross-pack composition proof:** separately test integrating selected pack versions with rollback and measured adaptation.
- **Cross-proposal causal outcome:** fair E0/E1/E2 and architecture-scoped comparisons, including total controller/pack/benchmark/integration cost.

## Honest negative results
A failed implementation can remain an EXPERIMENTAL or REJECTED pack with actual code/traces and reproduction of its failure; a no-code insight can be a valid RESEARCH pack with question, method, evidence and falsifier. Don't force failed work to look operational. Preserve secrets outside the publication and store sensitive raw evidence as restricted hash-addressed references.

## Gate
Publishing complete immature portfolios is mandatory; independently claiming VERIFIED/PORTABLE/COMPOSED without evidence is prohibited. A judge can downgrade claims and publish a corrected result without overwriting history.
