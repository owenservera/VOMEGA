# 03 — Actual Independent Development Systems

**This is not a folder of architecture authors.** These are the real agentic execution environments that design, test, code, dogfood, benchmark and emit versioned capability-pack portfolios. A design proposal is a seed; a development system is the running or independently launchable research/build agent and its isolated state.

## Two fundamentally distinct execution paths

### General Z.ai exploration lab
[general-zai-lab](general-zai-lab/README.md): the existing Z.ai Full-Stack initiative, previously housed in ../BOOTSTRAP-ZAI-EXPERIMENT-LAB/, is the **architecture-agnostic exploration track**. It retains original A and B competing strategies, the one-round-behind ecosystem challenger C, a prescribed four-plus-two fan-out and independent Truth benchmarking; the presence of these instructions is **not proof that live parallel agents or a remote Z.ai session are running**. **Existing Round 1 must not be restarted or silently respecified.** It need not endorse the O1 architecture. Its current launch prompt remains at ../BOOTSTRAP-ZAI-EXPERIMENT-LAB/01_PRIMARY_BOOTSTRAP_PROMPT.md.

### One independent Z.ai session for EACH unique proposal
[proposal-specific](proposal-specific/README.md): instantiate one full, separate top-level Z.ai builder/controller per accepted versioned architecture proposal. It receives only **00 baseline + 01 deliverables + its own anchored proposal** and runs its own code/test research and four-or-more genuine child experiments. It is constrained to test/improve/falsify the assigned proposal, not to become another unbounded general lab. If it learns a substantively different architecture would be superior, preserve a visible anchor control, label the derived new design and submit it separately with lineage.

[O1 / ours Z.ai](proposal-specific/ours-zai/BOOTSTRAP_PROMPT.md) is the first explicitly designed instance. Claude and future authors get **new independent sessions and workspaces** only after their actual proposals are submitted. No two unique proposals should share one model context or tool worktree just to save setup time; share audited static utilities and released evidence only when explicitly justified.

## Universal DNA research is cross-track, not a replacement developer system

The candidate [Elephant genome seed](../05_UNIVERSAL_ELEPHANT_DNA/README.md) is available to all new architecture studies as **optional explicitly versioned research context**. Its 20 proposed genes and six phenotype tests are challengeable; they are not imposed retroactively on active general, O1 or Claude rounds. The [Semantic Factory](semantic-factory-zai/README.md) is one proposed workload to pressure-test that DNA alongside unrelated domains.

## All paths inherit the same hard requirements
- Actual context-segregated subagents, distinct IDs and permissions, real workspace isolation, genuine model/tool calls and meaningful experimental fan-out.
- Four real isolated experiment arms minimum per bounded development round; the unrestricted general lab retains A1/A2/B1/B2 and C1/C2 six-arm rule. Dedicated proposal labs start with two internal competing refiners x two child arms and may introduce a lagged ecosystem challenger when justified; they do not **pretend** to have the general lab's A/B/C teams.
- Full lifecycle token/usage/time/performance and environment observability with UNKNOWN fields explicit.
- Frozen Ω seed and independent E0/E1/E2 comparisons; correct source, context, write and secret handling.
- **EVERY TOP-LEVEL DEVELOPMENT TURN:** publish a complete portfolio index of every owned capability pack, including discovered, experimental, blocked, rejected, retired and unchanged. Store real content with immutable references and verified provenance, not just maturity-passing packs.
- Per-session master source/evidence lineage and external clean-room handoff test.
- Single consolidated Git main for reviewed results; disposable isolation and no standing team branches.
- No claims of running agents until their real session IDs and evidence are observed.

## Avoid double-counting
General A/B/C are **internal research strategies**, not architecture-authors or the external dedicated proposal labs. E0/E1/E2 are **actual Elephant count**, not running agent headcount. A live Claude model can be an author, worker or Elephant, depending on distinct demonstrated role; model branding alone establishes none.

Publication/exchange is downstream of each lab's own release process, under ../04_TURN_PUBLICATIONS/. Cross-lab comparisons are independent and must record different budgets, versions and shared tools.

## Source-first dogfood development track

[dogfood-zai](dogfood-zai/README.md) is a prepared but **not launched** self-hosting lab that starts with *actual executable toolkits* from Elephant and the earlier ZAI/Claude output when their sanitized sources become accessible. It runs untouched baseline D0, minimal repair D1, integration D2 and competing replacement D3, preserving cohort isolation and full-turn releases. See its [seed registry](dogfood-zai/SEED_REGISTRY.json), [inventory script](dogfood-zai/inventory.mjs) and [starter](dogfood-zai/STREAM_STARTER.md). Artifact lists without mirrored files are not runnable seed code. This does not alter existing active labs or frozen experiments.
