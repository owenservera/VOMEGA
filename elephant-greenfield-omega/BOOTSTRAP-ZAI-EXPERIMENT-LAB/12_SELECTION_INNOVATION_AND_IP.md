# Tournament Selection, Learning Collapse, Novelty and IP Evidence

## Two masters must remain distinct

At the end of each round, Master A and Master B **each** receive a locked, unbiased report containing the results of all four arms, including opposite master's work. Master A independently proposes A-next and its retained/abandoned mechanisms; Master B independently proposes B-next. They must not jointly draft a single solution before independent assimilation. A later cross-pollination phase allows feature-level transfer with explicit source credit, but never deletes one master simply because the other won one short benchmark. Preserve two runnable, testable candidates and two living experiment roadmaps.

## Evidence-led selection

For every experiment, record preregistered hypothesis, expected effect, changed mechanism, actual setup, test task, runtime/model/seed, start/stop times, model/token/quota usage, sources/artifacts, errors, objective results, confounders, critic comments and independent judge disposition. A master may adopt a mechanism only if its claimed benefit survived checks or is explicitly flagged provisional for a new experiment. Prevent post-hoc changing the target metric after seeing results. Favor marginal verified improvement over elegant new complexity.

## Ranking

Compare: correctness and completeness (hard gate); verified throughput and critical-path latency (primary); causal influence of Elephant consultations (do the cited findings lead to accepted changes?); source trust/epoch accuracy; expense and model usage; operational fragility; novelty of mechanism; maintainability and reproducibility. Do not compress all dimensions into one opaque AI-written score. Publish a Pareto view: one approach may be faster while another is safer or easier to operate. Selection is per use case, with two masters retained for future rounds.

## Mechanism transfer and collapse protocol

Each master creates an evidence table with four dispositions: ADOPT NOW, ADAPT/TRIAL, REJECT, or DEFER. An adopted mechanism names the exact arm, patch/artifact, verified effect, caveats, and tests required to prevent regression. Before integration, compare against its own architecture and run a targeted non-regression suite. Preserve design lineage so later analysis can identify whether progress came from sustained Elephant residency, smarter context packs, evaluator changes, prompt variance or simple better engineering.

## Novelty register (research hypotheses only)

Candidate mechanisms include: (1) transaction-scoped context forks with verifiable flush; (2) Git-backed epoch-based memory validity; (3) cross-Elephant disagreement translated into executable falsifiers; (4) semantic dependency-triggered selective refresh; (5) dynamic cognitive domain split/merge based on utilization and error; (6) marginal-value routing between E0/E1/E2; (7) provable isolated child-agent execution; (8) evidence-locked master-approach competition; (9) cost-aware knowledge promotion from transient context; (10) context reconstruction with stable reasoning-calibration tests; (11) model/provider agnostic ECP envelopes; (12) agentic experimentation controls coupled to verified product value; (13) quality-adjusted context economics; (14) automated failure capsule to context update; (15) multi-master innovation migration with lineage retention.

For each candidate, conduct a prior-art search of academic, open-source and commercial implementations **at the time of claiming novelty**; record dates/links and differentiation. Novelty must be supported by technical distinctions and demonstrable outputs, not names, design diagrams or marketing. Do not call anything patentable without appropriate legal analysis. Keep an invention notebook with experiment run IDs, code commit IDs, dated evidence and contributor provenance.

## Pre-registration and statistical humility

Small early sample sizes cannot establish generalizable speedup. Include negative/null outcomes. Test repeated matched tasks with blind variations, run order randomization when practical, comparable model budgets and independent grading. Publish absolute counts, medians, variance, confidence limits when defensible, and known non-independence of repeated agent calls. Do not overinterpret single-round victories or token savings that fail to produce faster correct outputs.

## Additive build-vs-buy and prior-art comparison (2026-10-11)

Track C systematically checks claimed novel Elephant mechanisms against official protocol/framework behavior such as A2A, MCP, ACP, AG-UI, OpenTelemetry GenAI, LangGraph and Temporal, documenting spec versions, source, evidence, license, remaining novel ECP aspects and measured integration cost. A and B remain free to invent; C supplies credible prior art and proven reusable primitives. A/B may accept/reject those **independently in the next round**, never as retroactive mandate.
