# The Living Semantic Design Record

> **Owner-directed spatial parity clarification:** The shared Gym human-facing substrate is an effectively infinite 3D canvas, initially a floating text box; Machine/Human/Wiki/spatial views share one source-pinned real-time semantic/execution manifold with full parity as a testable target. Reuse [today's parity and spatial design](../../03_DEVELOPMENT_SYSTEMS/elephant-protocol-gym/INITIAL_STATE.md). WebAssembly is [only an optional experimental accelerator](../../03_DEVELOPMENT_SYSTEMS/elephant-protocol-gym/WASM_BOOSTER.md), not required.


**Light experimental launch:** [Elephant Protocol Gym](../../03_DEVELOPMENT_SYSTEMS/elephant-protocol-gym/README.md) is the practical four-arm starter. Its runs discover methods rather than inheriting the candidate WikiCore inventory as mandatory design.

**State:** ACTIVE FIRST PASS / REVIEWABLE. **Scope:** the Semantic SDK, compiler, runtime, self-describing source, wiki and intent projections, with its embedding in Elephant R&D. **Last substantive revision:** 2026-10-11.

This is the **evergreen, editable design source of record**, not a dated research report and not a declaration that speculative implementations are approved. It exists because the project was becoming too complicated to reconstruct from conversations, old proposals, overlapping specifications and experiments. A new collaborator should be able to learn the ambition in normal language, recognize what has actually been agreed, see what remains unknown and change one aspect of the design without rewriting everything else.

The core method is simple to state: begin with ordinary executable source code, make its meaning deliberately and even excessively explicit, validate those claims against the actual source and observations, and systematically experiment with compression and reconstruction. Build human-readable wiki, machine-readable representation and feedback into that first experiment. Explore alternative solutions with independently evaluated models and Elephants rather than guessing the final architecture.

**An engineer, language designer or computer scientist:** continue to [03 — The learning laboratory](03_FEEDBACK_AND_EXPERIMENTS.md) and [05 — Principles and contracts](05_PRINCIPLES_AND_CONTRACTS.md). **An Elephant researcher or agent-team designer:** read [04 — Elephant co-evolution](04_ELEPHANT_COEVOLUTION.md) after the shared design. This distinguishes hypotheses about memory and team cognition from established cognitive capabilities.

**An editor, reviewer or decision maker:** begin with [06 — Decision board](06_DECISION_BOARD.md) and [07 — How to change this record](07_EDITING_PROTOCOL.md). Consult [08 — Glossary](08_GLOSSARY.md) for consistent terminology.

## The record's authority — important

The [existing corpus precedence policy](../../CANONICAL_DEVELOPMENT_CORPUS.md) and [owner decision intake](../../DECISION_PROCESS_TRACKER.md) remain authoritative about *who can approve what*. The [existing Rule 1](../RULE_1_MACHINE_HUMAN_COEVOLUTION.md) remains the governing single Machine ↔ Human learning principle. This living record translates that direction into a coherent, readable design; it neither invents earlier approvals nor substitutes for live test evidence.

The **latest explicitly scoped owner decision** takes precedence, except that source truth and safety boundaries cannot be erased by documentation. Only decisions with a linked authority record may be marked OWNER_CONFIRMED. A design description may instead say OWNER_DIRECTION, PROVISIONAL_SYNTHESIS, CANDIDATE, OPEN, or REJECTED; those words have precise meanings in [07](07_EDITING_PROTOCOL.md). The first pass is an **editorial synthesis for confirmation**, not a blanket approval of every sentence.

## Fast changes

To edit a concept, find its stable identifier in [01](01_AGREED_STATE.md) or [06](06_DECISION_BOARD.md), change its owning file, state whether the claim is confirmed or speculative, describe the reason and source, then adjust the links it affects. Use the worked example and change checklist in [07](07_EDITING_PROTOCOL.md). **Do not rename these files for each revision**; Git carries the time history, while stable document paths preserve agent links.

The first useful outcome is **not** unanimous agreement on a stack. It is a small, durable record of what we mean, what we believe, what we have measured and what must be tested next.
