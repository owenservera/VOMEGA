## Classification law

- **ACTIVE_AUTHORITY:** current and executable/current instruction within its bounded scope. Check source revision before following.
- **LIVE_EXPERIMENT:** active independent proposal/agent session, capability pack or experiment; allowed to disagree but must not overwrite common product authority.
- **REFERENCE_EVIDENCE:** original source code, benchmark, historical fixture, immutable experiment or seed truth; available and provenance-bound, never deleted just for cleanup.
- **ARCHIVED_REVIEW:** shifted into a dated chunk because obsolete, conflicting, duplicated or uncertain; available for later decision, not a rejection.
- **QUARANTINE_PENDING:** cannot move safely yet because active references, script or legal/source custody dependency, or current run status is unknown. Index it and prevent default instruction adoption by precedence.
- **OUT_OF_SCOPE_ASK_OWNER:** outside `.project/` or `elephant-greenfield-omega/`; no relocation or mutation without explicit review.
