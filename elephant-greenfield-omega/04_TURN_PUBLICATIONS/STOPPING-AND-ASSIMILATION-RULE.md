# Elephant research stopping and assimilation rule

1. **No ten-round completion mandate.** Current ZAI/Claude efforts may be concluded at any turn. Every bounded turn is a potentially final research harvest.
2. **Preserve before stopping:** all-state PORTFOLIO, executable or partial toolkits, configs with secrets removed, schemas, tests, benchmarks, fixture hashes, negative results, run logs, decisions and precise source/epoch provenance.
3. **Discoverability first:** maintain a stable index by track/round/turn and separately index **tools by what they do**, with exact file path, maturity state, dependencies and invocation/verification notes.
4. **Technical assimilation, not indiscriminate merging:** record findings into Elephant shared intelligence; portable components require compatibility tests and provenance before adoption. No automatic changes to MVP or D1 implementation.
5. **Independent evidence rule:** reported tests stay AUTHOR_REPORTED until replayed, with experimental UNKNOWN and negative outcomes fully preserved.
6. **Stopping criterion:** continue only for a preregistered question whose answer could materially change an Elephant architectural decision. An unexplored research roadmap is not an obligation to run.
7. **Repository hygiene:** no credentials, raw private conversations or live browser cookies in public artifact folders. Prefer source+manifest and hashed sensitive evidence references.
8. **No maturity filtering:** retain experimental/blocked/rejected toolkits when informative; their listing does not imply endorsement.
